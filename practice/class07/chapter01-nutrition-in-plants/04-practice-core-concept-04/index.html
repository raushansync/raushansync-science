After This Core Concept

Food formation in plants is not a simple mixing of materials — it is a transformation powered by energy.

  • Carbon dioxide and water are raw materials, not food.
  • Sunlight provides energy but does not become part of the food.
  • Chlorophyll is essential because it captures light energy.
  • Without energy capture, food formation cannot occur even if materials are present.

Photosynthesis produces carbohydrate where energy becomes stored as chemical energy. Oxygen is released during this process, but it is a by-product rather than the main goal.

Understanding photosynthesis means understanding how life converts energy from the Sun into usable matter that supports growth, repair, and all food relationships.
