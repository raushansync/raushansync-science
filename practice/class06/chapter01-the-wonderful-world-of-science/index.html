Section D: Short Answer Questions

1. Define Science in your own words based on the chapter.

(Hint: Think about observing and understanding the world)

Science is a way of thinking, observing, and doing things to uncover the secrets of the universe and understand how things work.

2. List the steps of the "Scientific Method" in the correct order.

(Hint: Start with "Observation")

Observe → Ask a Question → Guess an Answer → Test the Guess → Analyse Results.

3. Why is science compared to a "Jigsaw Puzzle" in the text?

Because every new discovery adds another piece to the puzzle, but there is no limit to the puzzle; new pieces lead to more questions.

4. Give one example from the text of a daily life situation where someone acts like a scientist.

A person cooking checking why dal spilled (too much water?), or an electrician checking why a bulb isn't working.

Section E: Application & Critical Thinking

1. The "Pen" Experiment

Imagine you are writing an exam and your pen suddenly stops working. Describe how you would use the Scientific Method to solve this problem. Write down what you would observe, guess, and test.

  • Observation: The pen stopped writing.
  • Question: Why did it stop?
  • Guess 1: _________________
  • Test 1: _________________
  • Guess 2 (if Guess 1 was wrong): _____________________
Guess 1: The ink is finished. Test 1: Open the pen and check the refill. Guess 2: The ink has dried up (if the refill is full). (Your answer may vary.)
