RaushanSYNC Science

Hi, I'm Raushan.

I created this website because I believe science should be understood from first principles, not memorised for examinations. Every law, equation, and theory has a story behind it, and I want to help you discover that story through observation, questioning, experimentation, and reasoning.

Here you'll find carefully designed articles, video lessons, hands-on labs, and practice exercises that focus on building genuine understanding. Whether we're studying Physics, Chemistry, Biology, or Environmental Science, my aim is always the same: to help you understand why something works before asking you to remember it.

This website is designed for curious learners, from students taking their first science classes to anyone who wants to revisit the fundamentals with a deeper perspective. I believe strong foundations are built through curiosity, logical reasoning, and connecting ideas instead of memorising isolated facts.

I'm currently pursuing a BS in Data Science and Applications at IIT Madras, and building RaushanSYNC has become one of my ways of learning. Every lesson I write encourages me to study more carefully, ask better questions, and explain ideas as clearly as I can. As this website grows, I hope we can continue learning together.

Thank you for visiting. I hope the lessons on this site inspire you to stay curious, think scientifically, and discover the joy of understanding the world one concept at a time.
