After Core Concept 1

Food is not just for removing hunger — it supplies both energy and matter that keep life running.

  • All living organisms need food — not only for growth, but for constant repair and maintenance.
  • Life is defined by internal activity, not visible movement.
  • Food is both fuel and building material.

Plants are not “less alive” because they do not move — they are internally active at every moment.

If something is alive, it must require food in some form.
