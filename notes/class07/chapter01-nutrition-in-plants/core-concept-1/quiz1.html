After Completing Core Concept 1

Now you have understood that food is not merely something that removes hunger — it is the fundamental source of both energy and matter that keeps life running every moment.

  • Growth is not the only reason organisms need food.
  • Even a fully grown organism must constantly repair, regulate, and maintain itself.
  • Life is defined by internal activity — not visible movement.
  • Food is both fuel and building material.
  • Every living system is a self-maintaining system, and maintenance always consumes resources.

This idea is powerful.

It prevents a very common misunderstanding:

Plants are not “less alive” just because they do not walk or run.
They too are continuously active internally.

If something is alive, it must require food in some form.

And if something does not require food at all — it cannot be alive.

You have now built the foundation on which the entire chapter on Nutrition in Plants will stand.

From here onward, the important question changes:
