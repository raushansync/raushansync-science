After Core Concept 3

Making food internally does not mean being completely independent.

  • Autotrophs prepare their own food from simple substances like air and water.
  • They depend on sunlight and materials from their surroundings.
  • Their independence is from other organisms, not from the environment.

Because they manufacture food, autotrophs form the base of almost all food relationships. The food they produce becomes the starting point for other life forms.

Autotrophic nutrition is not just a plant feature — it is the foundation of nutritional connections in nature.
