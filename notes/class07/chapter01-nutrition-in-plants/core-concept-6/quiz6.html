After This Core Concept

Nitrogen completes the picture of plant nutrition that photosynthesis alone cannot explain.

  • Plants need nitrogen to produce proteins and vitamins — not just energy.
  • Although nitrogen gas is abundant in air, plants cannot use it directly.
  • Soil bacteria convert nitrogen gas into soluble forms that roots can absorb.
  • Some bacteria live freely in soil; others live inside legume roots in a symbiotic relationship.
  • Farmers add nitrogen-rich fertilisers to replenish what crops remove from the soil.

This means plant nutrition is never an isolated process — it connects air, soil, microorganisms, and farming into a single system.

Understanding nitrogen also helps explain why crop rotation works, why legumes improve soil fertility, and why soil quality matters as much as sunlight and water for healthy plant growth.
