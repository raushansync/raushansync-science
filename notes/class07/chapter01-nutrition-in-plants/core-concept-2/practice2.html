After Core Concept 2

Needing food is universal — but the way food is obtained is not.

  • Some organisms prepare their own food from simple, non-living substances.
  • Others cannot do this and must depend on ready-made food.
  • This difference is not about being a plant or an animal, but about nutritional ability.

No organism exists in isolation. Even those that make their own food form the base on which others survive.

Nutrition, therefore, is not just a personal need — it is a system of relationships.
