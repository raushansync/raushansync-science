After This Core Concept

Not all plants follow the same nutritional path — and that is precisely what makes plant nutrition a richer topic than it first appears.

  • Parasitic plants lack chlorophyll and take ready-made food from a living host, harming it in the process.
  • Insectivorous plants are green and photosynthesise for energy, but trap insects to obtain nutrients like nitrogen from poor soil.
  • Saprotrophic organisms grow on dead and decaying matter, break it down, and return nutrients to the environment.
  • Being a plant does not automatically mean being fully autotrophic — nutrition depends on ability and environment.

These exceptions do not break the rules of nutrition — they show how living things adapt their nutritional strategies when conditions make the usual way difficult or impossible.

Saprotrophs, in particular, remind us that even organisms that do not make food play an essential role in keeping nature balanced by recycling nutrients back into the soil.
