Section C: Critical Thinking & Analysis

9. The “Infinite Puzzle” of Science

The text states:

“There is no limit to what we can discover, since every new piece of knowledge leads to more questions.”

Question:

Explain why this makes science different from a normal jigsaw puzzle that you buy from a shop.

A normal jigsaw puzzle has a fixed number of pieces and one final picture. Science is different because it has no final picture. Every new discovery leads to more questions, so science is described as unending.

10. Analyzing Variables (Pen Scenario)

In the pen experiment, you check only the ink level first.

Question:

Why is it important to test one factor at a time instead of changing everything at once?

If multiple things are changed at once, we cannot know which change caused the result. Science requires testing one factor at a time to reach correct conclusions.

11. Case Study: The Withering Plant

Scenario:

  • Observation: Leaves are yellow
  • Question: Why are the leaves turning yellow?
  • Guess 1: The plant is not getting enough water
  • Test 1: She waters it daily for one week
  • Result: Leaves turn more yellow and begin to droop

Questions:

a) Was Guess 1 correct? Give a reason.

b) Propose one new guess that Riya should test next.

(a) Was Guess 1 correct?
No. If watering the plant made the condition worse, the guess was incorrect.

(b) Proposed Guess 2:

  • The plant may be getting too much sunlight
  • The soil may lack nutrients
  • The plant may have pests or disease

Explanation:
When one guess fails, the scientific method requires forming the next logical guess and testing it.
